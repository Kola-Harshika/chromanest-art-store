Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding original artworks, packaging, custom orders, and framing.

Are the artworks original or reproductions?
All pieces in our Paintings, Resin Art, Wall Art, and Handmade categories are 100% original, one-of-a-kind studio creations. Each comes with a signed and embossed Certificate of Authenticity. Our Art Prints are limited archival museum-grade reproductions strictly capped and numbered.
How are fragile artworks packaged and insured for shipping?
We use custom shockproof wooden crates and acid-free archival wrapping materials engineered specifically for fine art transport. Every shipment is fully insured for its full appraisal value via premium air couriers with real-time tracking.
Can I commission an artwork with specific dimensions or color palettes?
Yes! We frequently collaborate with private collectors and interior designers on custom commissions. Simply select "Custom Artwork Commission" in the form above and include your desired size and color moodboard. We provide color sketches for your approval before starting.
What is your return and satisfaction policy?
We want you to feel complete harmony with your artwork. If you are not delighted with your purchase, you may initiate a return within 14 days of receipt for a full refund or studio exchange.
Do you offer trade discounts for interior designers and architects?
Yes, our Trade & Hospitality Program provides exclusive tier discounts, complimentary swatch samples, and priority commissions for certified interior designers, staging firms, and architects.
